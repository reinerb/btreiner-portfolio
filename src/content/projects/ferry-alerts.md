When the ferry cancels a trip on short notice, riders need to hear about it before they leave for the dock. I built a system that lets office staff post an alert to the website, social media, and about 4,400 text subscribers from one screen. Moving the texts from a WordPress plugin to a custom Twilio integration cut delivery from about an hour and a half to about twenty minutes.
