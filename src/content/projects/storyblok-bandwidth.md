A client's Next.js and Storyblok site had high hosting costs for a long time, and nobody had found out why. I traced it to images that were skipping Next.js's image optimization. After the fix, bandwidth dropped from about 40 GB a day to about 3 GB, which saved the client roughly $75–150 a month.
